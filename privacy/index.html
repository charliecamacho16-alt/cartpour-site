CartPour privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the app stores

CartPour keeps your choices only on your device: the ingredients you tick, your favorite drinks, and your settings (theme, language, units and sort order). They are saved in the app's own storage, are never sent to us, and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What the app collects

Nothing. CartPour has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Network use

All drinks, recipes and fonts are built into the app, so it makes no network requests and works offline. Links to other websites open in your browser. If you share or copy a recipe, it goes only where you send it.

Alcohol

CartPour contains cocktail recipes and is intended for adults of legal drinking age where they live. Please drink responsibly. The Zero Proof page has non-alcoholic drinks.

Children

CartPour is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: main@cartpour.com.
