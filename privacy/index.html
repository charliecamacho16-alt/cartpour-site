CartPour privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 (GoatCounter page counts added)

What the app stores

CartPour keeps your choices only on your device: the ingredients you select, your favorite drinks, and your settings (theme, language, units and sort order). They are saved in the app's own storage (in the web version, your browser's local storage), are never sent to us, and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What the app collects

The Android app collects nothing. CartPour has no account, no advertising and no cross-site tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Network use

All drinks, recipes and fonts are built into the app, so the Android app makes no network requests and works offline. The web app works offline too; when online it also sends the GoatCounter page count described below. Links to other websites open in your browser. If you share or copy a recipe, it goes only where you send it.

Website and cookies

cartpour.com, including the web app at cartpour.com/app, counts page views with GoatCounter: which page was opened, the referring site, browser, screen size and country. GoatCounter sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and cannot identify you, so no consent banner is needed. The site sets no cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. The web app at cartpour.com/app stores your choices only in your browser, as described above.

Alcohol

CartPour contains cocktail recipes and is intended for adults of legal drinking age where they live. Please drink responsibly. The Zero Proof page has non-alcoholic drinks.

Children

CartPour is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: main@cartpour.com. See also the terms of use.
