Margarita

Citrusy · Sour · Refreshing

Ingredients

  • 1⅔ oz (50 ml) tequila
  • ⅔ oz (20 ml) triple sec
  • ½ oz (15 ml) lime juice
  • Garnish: salt or tajín rim

Steps

  1. Add everything to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake well and strain into a chilled coupe.
  3. Salt half the rim of the glass if you like.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your shelf, favorites and settings are saved in your browser or the app on your device, and nowhere else.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in the browser. Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”; it then opens like an app and works offline. The Android app is coming to Google Play.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every recipe is built in. Once you have opened the web app (or installed it), it keeps working offline.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes: Settings → Language → Español. A few of the newest labels are still in English.

Who is it for?

Adults of legal drinking age where they live, and anyone who wants a good mocktail. Please drink responsibly.
